Manas Ranjan Majhi is the founder of two companies at the intersection of executive hiring and operational infrastructure.

Majhi Group is a retained executive search firm placing VP and C-suite leaders for high-stakes organizations. With 25+ placements completed at an average of 30–45 days — against an industry median of 65–90 days — the firm operates a system-driven approach to search that most agencies cannot replicate.

Majhi OS is autonomous hiring operations infrastructure: a system that detects failing mandates before they collapse, predicts stalls weeks in advance, and executes recovery without manual orchestration. Think Datadog for hiring systems.

Manas grew up in Kalahandi, Odisha — one of India's most underserved districts — and built both companies from that foundational understanding that talent is abundant but opportunity is not. He writes on opportunity, India, technology, and systems that shape human potential at manasmajhi.com.
