There are places that appear on maps.
And then there are places that live inside people.
For me, Odisha is both.
Whenever people ask where I am from, the answer is simple.
Odisha.
But the meaning behind that answer becomes more difficult to explain.
Because Odisha is not simply a location.
It is a set of memories.
A set of values.
A way of seeing the world.
It is where I learned my first lessons about life.
Where I learned resilience.
Where I learned humility.
Where I learned that ambition and simplicity can coexist.
Like many people who grow up outside major economic centers, I spent part of my life looking outward.
Toward larger cities.
Toward bigger opportunities.
Toward places that seemed to matter more.
It is a common experience.
You grow up hearing stories about success happening somewhere else.
The most important companies are somewhere else.
1

The biggest opportunities are somewhere else.
The future is somewhere else.
For a while, you start believing it.
Then life teaches you something interesting.
Places do not become important on their own.
People make them important.
Every thriving city was built by people who decided their home was worth investing in.
Every great institution was built by people who cared enough to stay, return, or contribute.
Every success story begins with someone believing that improvement is possible.
That realization changed how I think about Odisha.
I stopped asking:
"What can Odisha do for me?"
And started asking:
"What can people like me do for Odisha?"
The answer is not always obvious.
It does not require politics.
It does not require enormous wealth.
It does not require extraordinary influence.
Sometimes it starts with something simpler.
Belief.
Belief that the state has potential.
Belief that talent exists here.
Belief that opportunity can be created here.
2

Belief that the future can be different from the past.
Odisha is often underestimated.
Those who know it only through statistics rarely understand it.
Those who experience it understand something else entirely.
They discover generosity.
Culture.
History.
Resilience.
Warmth.
Community.
A quiet confidence that does not always seek attention.
I have had the privilege of traveling to different places and meeting people from different backgrounds.
Every journey teaches something valuable.
Yet travel has also reinforced my appreciation for home.
Distance creates perspective.
The further you go, the more clearly you see where you came from.
And every time I return, I am reminded that Odisha's greatest strength is not its resources.
Not its geography.
Not even its history.
Its people.
Because talent is everywhere.
Potential is everywhere.
The challenge has always been creating enough opportunities for that potential to flourish.
3

That is why I care about entrepreneurship.
Why I care about education.
Why I care about technology.
Why I care about talent.
Because each of those areas creates opportunity.
And opportunity changes lives.
The future of Odisha will not be determined by a single government.
A single company.
Or a single leader.
It will be shaped by thousands of people making decisions every day.
Students choosing to dream bigger.
Founders choosing to build.
Professionals choosing to mentor.
Citizens choosing to contribute.
Small actions compound.
Over time, they become movements.
Over time, they become ecosystems.
Over time, they become transformation.
I do not believe Odisha needs to become another version of somewhere else.
It does not need to become the next Bengaluru.
Or the next Singapore.
Or the next anything.
It needs to become the best version of itself.
4

Confident in its identity.
Proud of its culture.
Open to innovation.
Committed to opportunity.
That is a future worth building.
And that is why I continue talking about Odisha.
Not because I believe it is perfect.
Because I believe it is possible.
There is a difference.
Love is not ignoring imperfections.
Love is seeing potential clearly and caring enough to help realize it.
This is my home.
This is where my story began.
And no matter where the future leads, a part of me will always be working toward a simple goal:
Helping create more opportunities for the people and the place that helped create me.
Thank you, Odisha.
For the lessons.
For the values.
For the perspective.
For the journey.
And for reminding me that where you begin does not limit where you can go.
5

Learning Outcome
True pride in a place does not come from believing it is perfect. It comes from recognizing its potential and
contributing to its progress.
