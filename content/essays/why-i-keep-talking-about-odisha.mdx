People occasionally ask me a simple question.
"Why do you keep talking about Odisha?"
The question usually comes from a good place.
After all, there are countless topics to discuss.
Business.
Technology.
Artificial intelligence.
Global markets.
Startups.
Leadership.
Yet somehow, I often find myself returning to Odisha.
The answer is surprisingly simple.
Because Odisha is not just where I am from.
It is part of how I see the world.
Every person is shaped by something.
A place.
A family.
A community.
A set of experiences.
For me, Odisha shaped far more than my geography.
It shaped my perspective.
1

Growing up, I did not think much about it.
Home is simply home when you are young.
You do not analyze it.
You live it.
Only later do you begin noticing the lessons it quietly taught you.
Patience.
Resilience.
Humility.
Resourcefulness.
Community.
The ability to find joy in simple things.
These values become part of you long before you recognize them.
As I grew older and traveled more, something interesting happened.
The further I went, the more clearly I saw where I came from.
Travel has a way of doing that.
It broadens your perspective while deepening your appreciation for home.
You begin comparing.
Not in terms of superiority.
In terms of understanding.
You notice what different places value.
You notice what they prioritize.
You notice what they celebrate.
And eventually, you begin asking larger questions.
2

Why do some regions develop faster than others?
Why do some places attract talent while others lose it?
Why do opportunities cluster in certain locations?
What would it take to change that?
Those questions matter because development is not evenly distributed.
Opportunity is not evenly distributed.
Talent exists everywhere.
Access does not.
I have seen capable people from Odisha achieve extraordinary things when given the right opportunities.
Not because they became different people.
Because the environment changed.
The visibility changed.
The access changed.
The opportunities changed.
That observation stayed with me.
Because it revealed something important.
The challenge is often not talent.
The challenge is creating pathways.
This is one reason I care about Odisha's future.
Not because I believe Odisha is perfect.
Every place has challenges.
Every region has unfinished work.
The question is whether people care enough to help improve it.
3

Criticism is easy.
Contribution is harder.
I prefer contribution.
Sometimes contribution means building businesses.
Sometimes it means creating opportunities.
Sometimes it means sharing ideas.
Sometimes it simply means talking about possibilities.
Possibility matters.
The future is shaped by what people believe is possible.
A generation that believes growth is possible behaves differently.
A generation that believes opportunity is possible behaves differently.
A generation that believes progress is possible behaves differently.
Optimism is not blind positivity.
It is the belief that improvement is achievable.
That belief changes behavior.
And behavior changes outcomes.
Perhaps that is why I continue talking about Odisha.
Because I see potential.
Potential in its people.
Potential in its cities.
Potential in its institutions.
Potential in its future.
I do not see a finished story.
4

I see a story that is still being written.
One that can move in many directions.
One that deserves attention.
One that deserves belief.
Some people talk about places only after they become successful.
I think it is more interesting to talk about them while they are still becoming.
Because becoming is where the real story lives.
That is why I keep talking about Odisha.
Not because it is where my journey began.
Because I believe some of its most important chapters have not been written yet.
And I would like to help write a few of them.
Learning Outcome
The places we come from shape how we see the world. Real pride is not celebrating a place only after it
succeeds—it is believing in its potential while it is still becoming.
