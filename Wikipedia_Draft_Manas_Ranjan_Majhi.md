'''Manas Ranjan Majhi''' (also known as '''Maanas Ranjan Majhi''' and '''Manas Majhi''') is an Indian entrepreneur and executive search professional based in India. He is the founder of [[Majhi Group]], a retained executive search firm specialising in VP and C-suite placements, and [[Majhi OS]], a company building autonomous hiring operations infrastructure. Majhi grew up in [[Kalahandi district|Kalahandi]], [[Odisha]], and attended a government Odia-medium school before building his career in executive search and technology entrepreneurship.

==Career==

===Majhi Group===
Majhi Group is a retained executive search firm founded by Manas Ranjan Majhi. The firm focuses on VP and C-suite placements, operating on a retained search model. As of 2024, the firm has completed more than 25 C-suite and VP placements.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://manasmajhi.com/work |title=Work — Manas Majhi |website=manasmajhi.com |access-date=2026-06-11}}</ref>

===Majhi OS===
Majhi OS is a technology company founded by Manas Majhi that develops autonomous hiring operations infrastructure. The company's products are designed to monitor mandate health, predict hiring system failures, and execute recovery actions without manual intervention.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://majhi.tech |title=Majhi OS |website=majhi.tech |access-date=2026-06-11}}</ref>
