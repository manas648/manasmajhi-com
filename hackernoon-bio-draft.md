### About / company description (longer bio)

Majhi Group is a retained executive search firm that places VP and C-suite leaders for high-growth technology companies and organizations that cannot afford to get the hire wrong.

Founded by Manas Majhi (Manas Ranjan Majhi), the firm operates a structured search process — not contingency volume — with a documented average of 30–45 days from engagement to offer against an industry median of 65–90. The most cited proof point: a $275K VP of Sales mandate that two other firms failed to close in 60+ days. Majhi Group closed it in 41.

The retained model is deliberate. Contingency search creates incentives that are misaligned with client outcomes — speed of closure, not quality of fit. Retained search aligns the firm's success with the client's: the right person, in the right context, at the right time. Majhi Group's 90-day replacement guarantee reflects that alignment.

The firm has completed 25+ C-suite and VP placements. 90%+ offer acceptance rate. Weekly status reports on every active mandate.

Manas Majhi writes on executive search, hiring operations, and opportunity at manasmajhi.com.
