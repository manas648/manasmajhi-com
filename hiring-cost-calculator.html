How the Cost Is Calculated

The vacancy cost model combines four components weighted by role type and days open. Lost revenue is calculated as daily OTE × a revenue multiplier (0.5× for support functions, 1.2× for moderate roles, 2.5× for high-revenue roles, 4× for critical revenue ownership) × days vacant. This reflects the real output gap — not just salary cost.

The recruiting fee is straightforward: OTE × fee percentage, typically 20–25% for setained executive search. Management overhead is calculated as 250 days per year × manager OTE of $350K (CEO/CPO typical) × hours per week spent covering the gap, divided by 40-hour weeks. Ramp cost reflects that new VP hires typically operate at 50% productivity for the first 90 days — calculated as OTE × 50% × (90/365).

Past day 70, the model applies a 1.25× compounding multiplier to the revenue loss component. This reflects Majhi OS data showing that searches unresolved past week 10 generate accelerating hidden costs: team members permanently absorb the gap, hiring manager confidence erodes, and pipeline candidates lose momentum.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a vacant VP role cost per day?

For a $300K OTE role with moderate revenue dependency, the fully loaded daily cost is approximately $1,800–$2,400. For a direct revenue role (VP Sales, CRO), the daily cost can reach $4,000–$6,000.

What is included in the total vacancy cost?

Four components: (1) lost revenue or output attributable to the unfilled seat, (2) recruiting fee, (3) management overhead — the time senior leaders spend covering the gap, and (4) ramp cost — the productivity deficit during the first 90 days after hire.

Why does vacancy cost accelerate past 70 days?

Past day 70, three effects compound: recruiter attention shifts to newer mandates, hiring manager confidence drops, and team members begin permanently absorbing the gap. Majhi OS data shows 68% of VP searches that reach day 70 stall past 14 weeks without intervention.

How does Majhi OS reduce vacancy cost?

Majhi OS closes VP and C-suite searches in an average of 50 days versus a 14-week industry median. At $2,500/day fully loaded cost for a $300K OTE role, that 48-day gap recovers approximately $120,000 per mandate. The system also eliminates the $3,280/month fragmented tool stack most recruiting teams are running.

What is a realistic recruiting fee for executive search?

Retained executive search typically runs 20–25% of total first-year compensation. A $300K OTE role at 22% is $66,000. Contingency recruiters charge 15–18% but are incentivised toward speed not quality — and failed placements restart the clock at full cost.
